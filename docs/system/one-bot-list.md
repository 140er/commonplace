### One list of bots

In Deck, `#/bots` is the only list of bots. Each row opens that bot; the bot's
conversations and its setup steps are reached from the bot itself, through
"Conversations and setup" in its menu, which opens a page titled by the bot.
The old workspace-wide conversations address (`#/bots/-/coworkers`, and the
older `#/coworkers`) resolves to `#/bots` so existing links keep working;
links to a single conversation and to a bot's own conversations page are
unchanged. Opening a conversation, steering, sharing and adding a bot work
as before. Classic keeps its own layout and addresses.
