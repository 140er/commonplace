### Meeting-join bots

- `docs/design/meeting-join-bots.md` is the design note for inviting a bot into a video meeting (card #463). Decisions it proposes: one adapter interface per meeting provider; the bot joins as a visibly named non-person, announces itself in the meeting chat and listens only after the announcement is delivered; any host removal or "bot stop" discards what was captured; audio is transcribed locally and never written to disk; notes, decisions and actions are generated and labelled as such, readable only by the inviter, and filed or turned into cards only on the inviter's tap; in-meeting answers happen only when addressed and reach only the meeting's content plus one pinned project; everything is off until the owner, the person and the meeting each opt in. No code changed; slices follow one PR each.

- Slice 1 is built (`commonplace/meetings/`): the `meetings.sqlite3` data model, sealed provider credentials (write-only, never in a response, log or audit row), the owner's master, transcript and hosted-model switches with limits, provider connections that can only be switched on after a passing adapter test, each person's opt-in, `commonplace meetings status|disable` and a read-only `meeting_status` tool, over an adapter interface with an in-memory fake and no provider registered by default. Nothing joins or listens yet: inviting is refused for anyone who did not opt in and otherwise answers 501 until the lifecycle slice. The per-day workspace counter table of the design note arrives with the lifecycle slice that enforces it.
