### Card snooze

- A Backlog or Todo card can be snoozed until a time or until another card is done; it is hidden from work offers, never started, and wakes once (also after a restart) through a lazy wake on board reads plus a worker that polls every 30 seconds. Only the owner or the assignee snoozes. Free-text conditions and snoozing a thread are not built; a runner-configured board does not auto-start a card on wake (a wake is not a todo event).
