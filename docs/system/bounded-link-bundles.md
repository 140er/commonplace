### Bounded link bundles

- On the routed Compute canvas, same-row parallel links span at most 32 map units. Two links keep their previous 16-unit separation. Cross-row traffic with at least four links sharing a clear side corridor uses a 32-unit bundle with distinct strokes and ordered device exits. A corridor is used only when every replacement segment and status mark clears the labels and devices; otherwise the existing router remains in use.
- Each stroke remains independently selectable by pointer, Enter and Space. This rule adds no controls and leaves sparse routes and the classic canvas unchanged.
