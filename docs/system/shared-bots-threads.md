### Shared team bots: shared threads and the audience rule

- Slice S4 of `docs/design/shared-team-bots.md` (card #462) replaces `409 not_available`: a member with `talk` opens a shared thread, read by the owner and every member with `see_shared` from their own `history_from`, and a member with `talk` alone reads only threads they opened. Private threads are unchanged and stay private from everyone but their person, the owner included.

- The audience rule is enforced where reach is computed: a turn in a shared thread uses `bot access ∩ asker ∩ owner ∩ every current reader`, from fresh principals at turn start and on every tool call, and the model is only offered, told and handed what all of them may use. A reader who cannot be checked fails the turn closed; a person who left the workspace reads nothing and no longer narrows it. If a narrower reader joins while a turn runs, the stored reply is a fixed sentence (`failed:audience`). Refusals use the fixed text "I can't use that in a shared thread." and never name a project.

- Decisions taken from the note's recommendations or chosen here: writes need only the asker's, the owner's and the bot's write right (readers do not write, but must read the project); a shared-thread model reads only bot-level notes and no learned items; the context carries only messages newer than the newest reader's `history_from`; events and forwarded answers carry no titles or text; at most 100 open shared threads per bot (not in the note); only the asker stops their own turn. Question 2 of the note (private-only first) is answered by this slice being built behind the same workspace switch, which stays off by default.
