### Meeting answers

- In-meeting answers (`commonplace/meetings/answers.py`, card #463 slice 5) reply in the meeting chat only to a message the provider marked as addressed to the bot, on the inviter's scope narrowed to the meeting's own content and at most one pinned project at the chosen disclosure (`none` by default). The model gets that content as quoted data and no tools, so nothing can be written from chat: a request to add something is kept as a proposed action for the inviter to approve later. Assumptions taken from the design note's recommendations: the surface is a thin call path on the configured chat model and the shared per-person model budget, not a hidden bot thread; a pinned project the inviter cannot read is refused at every disclosure level, including `none`; every link in a reply is removed (stricter than "unless they point to the hub"); messages that are not addressed are never stored, and the journal keeps only addressed ones (answered, refused, ignored by cooldown or cap, or failed); the 20-answer cap counts refusals; a hosted model is refused unless the owner allowed it for meetings.
