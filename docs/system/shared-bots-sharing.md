### Shared team bots: sharing and members' private threads

- Slice S3 of `docs/design/shared-team-bots.md` (card #462) adds the routes: the owner of a custom bot shares it with a current workspace member (`talk` and `see_shared` flags), changes the flags and revokes; a member then sees the bot in their roster (with its owner and their own permissions) and talks to it in their own private thread (`?thread=`, opened on first use). The workspace switch has `GET`/`PATCH /api/bots/settings` (read by any member, written by a workspace owner in a web session). Sharing stays off by default: with the switch off a share is refused (`409 sharing_off`) and members get 404 for every shared bot, without any share being changed.

- Privacy is enforced in the store: every thread read or write goes through `BotStore.reach`, so a member's thread is reachable only by that member. The owner, the workspace owner, another member, another workspace, an agent token and an app that changes sharing all get 404, and there is no route that lists another person's thread. A turn re-checks the share before every model request, every tool call and before its reply is stored; if it ended, the stored reply is the fixed `failed:revoked` sentence with no model text and nothing from a tool runs afterwards.

- Decisions taken from the note's recommendations: a member's notes are their own (`?own=1`) and the owner's are bot-level and read into every member's turn, never the reverse (question 4); a shared-bot turn counts against the talker's budget (question 3); shared threads are not creatable yet (`409 not_available`, question 2). Event messages for a filed task or a forwarded post go to the thread of the person whose turn filed it, and only while their share and rights still hold. Request ids are unique per thread; a turn's lease and action journal use a per-thread key so two members can never collide. A shared bot runs one turn per thread and at most four at once.
