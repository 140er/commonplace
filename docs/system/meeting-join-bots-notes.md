### Meeting-join bots: notes and filing (slice built)

- A meeting that ended keeping what it captured gets generated notes while it is `processing`: a summary, decisions, action items and open questions, each citing the segment ranges that show it. The model is the one the owner configured for chat, local unless the owner switched a hosted one on for meetings; it receives the transcript as quoted data and no tools. The server drops any item whose evidence does not exist and any attribution the cited segments do not carry. Nothing is stored when no usable model exists: the segments wait for a few retries and a one-day sweep.
- The notes are drafts, shown as generated with their model and evidence, readable only by the inviter. Segments are deleted as soon as the notes exist unless the owner allowed transcripts and the inviter kept this one. The inviter edits (the text is then theirs), dismisses, keeps a note in memory or approves an action as a card; each is an explicit tap, files once however often it is repeated, goes through the inviter's own write rights and carries a line saying it was proposed by the notes bot and who approved it. Deleting a meeting removes everything it owns and lists what was filed so it can be deleted too.
