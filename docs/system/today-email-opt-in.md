### Email for new Today items is opt-in and title-only

- `POST /api/today-email` saves the choice (`{"enabled": true|false}`) and `GET` reads it back. Phone push needs a notification server and covers the owner's home workspace only, so it never reached members or servers without one. Email reuses the sign-in mail sender (`COMMONPLACE_SMTP_HOST` and `COMMONPLACE_SMTP_FROM`) and is off until a person turns it on in Settings, under Email for Today. With no sender configured the setting says so and does nothing.
- A pass reads each opted-in person's own `needs_me`, so a person is only told about items they can read. The mail holds the card number, title and a link, never the body, notes or context (verbatim memory stays in the app). A burst is one mail; at most three mails per person and workspace per hour (the setting, the noted items and the cap are per workspace), the rest wait for a later mail. Residual: the title is written by a person or an agent and is mailed as written.
