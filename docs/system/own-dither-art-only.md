### Dithered Deck art is made only from our own images

- The project owner asked for the look of a public ASCII and dither art library in the Capy Deck. The library's terms allow its items as design reference only, so no item, sample image or generator output made from its images is downloaded, copied, traced or shipped, and the library is not named or linked in code or public text.
- Decision: the dithered art on empty and loading states is ours. `design/capy-art/make_sources.py` draws small grayscale source sprites from our own mark (the polygon in `icon.svg`) with plain gradients, and `scripts/capy_art.py` applies a 4x4 ordered Bayer threshold, a public algorithm, and writes `deck-art-data.js`. Sources and script are committed, so the art can be regenerated byte for byte; `tests/test_deck_art.py` pins that.
- The art is decorative: each piece sits in an `aria-hidden` element, holds no text, never animates, takes the surrounding text colour and is small (each piece under 4 KB, all together under 20 KB). The words beside it stay the accessible content.
- Rule for later slices: new art starts from a source image of ours added to `design/capy-art/`, never from a library item or an image we do not own. Anything from outside the repo is reference only.
- Not changed: copy, layout, routes, or any data the empty states read.
- Slice two adds seven more sprites from the same mark (page title row, section line, rail, four import steps); the generator and rule are unchanged.
