### Shared team bots: members and owners leaving

- Slice S5 of `docs/design/shared-team-bots.md` (card #462) builds section 9: a removed member's shares end and the bot, its shared history and their private thread stay (unreachable to everyone, the owner and the adopter included); re-adding them restores no share and no old history. A removed owner's bots are frozen: nobody can talk, members and apps get 404, nothing is deleted. A workspace owner lists every shared bot (no threads), may disable one (the same freeze, reversible) and may adopt one whose owner left.

- Decisions taken from the note's recommendations or chosen here: freeze, then a workspace owner adopts; the access list is kept and only re-capped, never widened, by evaluating it against the adopter's rights (question 5); adoption never opens the old owner's original thread (it stays behind a floor, because messages are append-only); a disabled bot is frozen for everyone including its owner, with `enable` to lift it (not in the note); adopt takes a shared custom bot only, so default bots and private bots of a removed person stay frozen and invisible; the control database has no member-removal path yet, so the hook (`lifecycle.member_removed`) is the entry every removal path calls, and a sweep on the roster and the workspace list covers a removal that skipped it (re-adding before either runs cannot be told apart, so removal paths must call the hook); an archived shared bot keeps its shares and takes no new turn, for its owner too.

- A bot that was disabled when its owner left becomes owner-left (still frozen), so `enable` refuses it and only an adoption lifts it. The hook never raises into the removal path; a failure is logged and the sweep on the roster and the workspace list (which runs on every listing) applies it later, so removal paths that can fail the hook should retry it.
