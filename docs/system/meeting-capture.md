### Meeting capture and local transcription

- Meeting capture (`commonplace/meetings/capture.py`, card #463 slice 3) turns the audio a provider hands over into transcript segments on the hub's own transcription engine, the one dictation uses. Audio lives in memory only and is dropped as soon as it is transcribed; windows are 20 s with a 2 s overlap, per speaker when the provider streams per speaker and mixed (no speaker id) otherwise. Assumptions taken from the design note's recommendations: an engine that falls behind by more than 60 s drops the audio with a recorded `(gap)` segment rather than catching up late; transcription needs only that voice is on and installed for the instance (not the workspace's dictation toggle) and otherwise inviting is refused with `needs_local_transcription`, with no service fallback; dictation keeps priority by being waited for between windows, not by pre-empting a window that is running; three consecutive failed windows end the meeting and discard it.
