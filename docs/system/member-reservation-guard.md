### Member reservation guard

- This section governs status moves; it supersedes the member sentence in the handoff bullet of the Board coordinator settings section for status-only updates. Signed-in members other than the owner cannot change the status of a todo or doing card reserved for another actor, on any route (update, web move or handoff). A status change covers take over, drop, hand off, complete (done) and send to review, and any other status change while the card is todo or doing. The refusal names only the workspace owner.
- Restating a card's current status is not a status change: a member's status-only update to `doing` on a card already held by another actor succeeds as a no-op, and the holder is unchanged.
