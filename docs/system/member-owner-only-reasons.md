### Members see why an owner-only control is missing

- Where an owner-only control would sit, a member sees "Only the workspace owner can change this." or a sentence that says who to ask. The line is screen copy only; server checks are unchanged and it names no counts, people, creators or trust levels. The list of controls that stay absent for a member is in `docs/invites.md`.
- Who may create a connection (token) is unchanged: only the workspace owner. Whether members may create their own is an open owner decision.
