- **Owns:** Setup proposals approved by the owner (card #675, `docs/system/setup-proposals.md`): `commonplace/projects/setups.py` (the additive `setup_proposals` table, `validate`, `canonical`, `digest`, the plain-words `changes` and `summarize`, `propose`, `show`, `listing`) and `commonplace/projects/setup_api.py` (the `setup_propose` and `setup_show` tools, `GET|POST /api/setup-proposals` and `GET /api/setup-proposals/<id>`).
- **Promises:** Only the workspace owner (the signed-in owner or a connection of the owner) is answered; members, their agents and machine credentials get the 404 of an unknown route and do not see the tools, and a connection never sees a proposal naming a project its policy cannot read. A proposal is a versioned row whose `sha256` is the digest of the stored canonical JSON (sorted keys, no spaces, UTF-8). Proposing writes only that row and its `setup.propose` audit entry; the same waiting body answers the existing version and at most 20 wait. Text that looks like a credential is refused, so no read holds one.
- **How it fails:** A bad setup is refused with a `code` and the field's `path` before anything is stored (`unknown_field`, `duplicate_key`, `unknown_bot`, `unknown_project`, `looks_secret`, `invalid`, `empty`, `too_large`); a full queue is `too_many_pending` (409). A project that is gone after proposing is marked `problem: unknown_project` in the read, never raised.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_setup_proposals.py`.
