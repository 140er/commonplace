- **Owns:** Who ranked a card. Each card records `priority_by` (`""`, `person`, `coworker`; public `priority_source` `default`, `person`, `coworker`) next to its `priority`, in `commonplace/boards/priority.py`, the store column and `BoardService.task_update`.
- **Promises:** A card made without a priority is `normal` with source `default`, never empty, and the board's Coworker ranks it on intake. A signed-in workspace owner sets a priority on any card and it becomes `person`; setting the current value still marks it as the person's. The board's Coworker (its coordinator or live lease holder) may set one only on a card no person ranked, and must pass `priority_reason` (one line, at most 200 characters), which lands in the card history as `Priority set to <name> by the Coworker: <reason>`. A person's priority is never replaced by a Coworker's. Task lines add `priority_by:person` or `priority_by:coworker` once someone ranked the card.
- **How it fails:** Any other caller, or a Coworker on a card a person ranked, gets a `no:` refusal and the card is unchanged. A missing, empty, multi-line or over-long reason, or a reason without a priority, is refused; a person passing a reason is refused. The column is added additively; on migration a non-default priority (only an owner could set one before) becomes `person`, and every other existing card reads as `default`.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_card_priority_ranking.py` and `tests/test_commonplace_card_priority.py`.
