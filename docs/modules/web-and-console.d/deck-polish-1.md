- **Owns:** `deck-today-capy.js` names the Today dialog for the button pressed (`replyWords`: Answer opens "Answer" with "Send answer", Reply opens "Reply"), shows one plain line under Approve (`approveNote`), and after a send shows a confirmation line (`sentLine`) that names what was sent, to whom and where the card went. `deck-shell.css` gives the phone mic a gap to the tabs; `deck-shell.js` draws no Project settings gear in the phone top bar. `board.js` marks the Add card key hint, `workbench.css` hides it on coarse pointers and narrow screens, and `deck-board.css` gives the phone Add card sheet an opaque Deck surface.
- **Promises:** On Today the dialog title matches the button ("Answer" or "Reply"), a successful send leaves a status line for about eight seconds, and the Approve button has a one-line explanation under it. At 390 px the mic is at least 8 px from the nearest tab and inside the bar, the phone chrome has exactly one Settings gear (the bottom bar's; a project's settings open from the board menu), the "Enter adds · Esc cancels" hint is hidden on coarse pointers and under 700 px, and the Add card sheet has a fully opaque background. Escape closes an open Move menu first and the card panel on the next press.
- **How it fails:** The confirmation line is local and clears itself, so a send that fails shows the existing error and no confirmation. A card whose bot has no name is described as "the coworker".
- **Tests:** `tests/test_deck_polish_browser.py` runs the real shell against a served fixture (skips where no browser is installed) and measures each item, red on the previous build; `tests/deck_home_today.mjs`, `tests/deck_shell.mjs` and `tests/commonplace_deck.mjs` pin the wording helpers, the mic track and the missing gear.
