- **Owns:** The card panel's Review comments section in `cardreviews.js`, the owner's project review settings in `reviewsettings.js`, and the Pipeline review chip and card link.
- **Promises:** Status, reason and next-step words come from the server; one persistent status line and stable control keys preserve announcements and focus. Members read the card's comments without owner controls. Project settings contain the hidden section markup and load its controller only when settings open, so importing the project's display helpers needs no browser dependencies. The card's settings link opens that panel through a reloadable route; Back and Escape return to the board.
  A fixed phone card panel scrolls its content inside the panel, keeping its header and composer clear of long review comments.
  An abandoned first read clears its loading placeholder without replacing a previously shown list.
  Decision announcements inspect `card_answered` strictly: Backlog is announced only after confirmation. A partial success says what changed for the comments, then explains the remaining unrelated question or refused card answer and directs the owner to the card. A missing confirmation never becomes a claimed move.
- **How it fails:** An unreadable card hides its section; unavailable reads explain how to try again, and a refused save keeps the last confirmed settings. The reader status never contains a credential.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_card_reviews_ui.py`, `tests/test_card_reviews_browser.py`, the standalone project-module harnesses in `tests/test_projects_own_wing.py`, `tests/test_projects_wing_edges.py` and `tests/test_projects_connect_state.py`, and the shell's node harnesses.
