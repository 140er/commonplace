- **Owns:** `deck-coworkers.js` also states what a Coworker does now or did last. It keeps Send off with a visible reason until a conversation is open and explains Steer, Chat and Private in one line each. `bot-setup.js` holds the one copy for a bot that cannot answer yet. `routes.js` exports `botPage`, the single address a bot opens from the Bots list and from the rail (`deck-shell.js`).
- **Promises:** The Working on panel opens with one line built from existing data only: a running or queued job reads "Working now" or "Queued", the newest finished job reads "Last ran" with its outcome, and no data reads "Idle". A job's card gets an "Open the card" link. Send and the draft stay disabled, with a described reason, until a conversation is open; Steer is blocked with its own reason while no task runs. A bot opens `#/bots/<id>`, or its Coworkers view when only the Coworker registry knows it, from every entry point. A missing chat model names the setting and where it lives, on the Bots list, the notice and the Coworker page.
- **How it fails:** Jobs of other conversations and progress the viewer may not read never reach the line or create a link. Missing data degrades to "Last activity" or "Idle", never to invented work. The no-model matching is limited to rows whose own summary says so.
- **Tests:** `deck_coworker_page_guidance.mjs` (run through `test_deck_coworkers.py`) covers each item in browser-free form, including the mounted page; `deck_coworkers.mjs` was updated for the single bot address and the idle line.
