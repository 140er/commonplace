- **Owns:** `release-block.js` draws the "Waiting for release" block at the top of a card waiting for release (creator, trust level, Release reason field, Release, Hold); `app.js` inserts it at the top of the card body in both looks, and `needs.js` shows the same creator line and the same one word in Today.
- **Promises:** Only a person who may release sees the block, and release is two clicks plus a reason: open the card, fill Release reason, press Release. Hold closes the panel and keeps the card waiting. The button, the form and the board all say Release. The Deck drawer no longer carries a separate Approve button for these cards.
- **How it fails:** A refused release shows the server's reason beside the form and the card stays waiting. A creator whose connection is gone shows "trust unknown" instead of a guess.
- **Tests:** `tests/release_block.mjs` (run through `tests/test_commonplace_release_in_today.py`) covers the block, the one word and its place in the card body; `tests/test_commonplace_release_in_today.py` covers Today with and without a connected Coworker, a member who does not see it, release through the existing path, and the creator and trust on the card read.
