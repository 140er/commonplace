- **Owns:**

  - `commonplace/web/static/compute-workspaces.js` and its section in `compute.js`/`compute.css`: the "Cloud workspaces" section of the Compute management screen, shared by Classic and the Deck Management tab. It lists records (name as a heading, one plain status, one resource line, buttons named per workspace), shows a form (name, image choice or own address, CPU cores, memory in GiB, storage, network, machine) with a resource preview, inline validation and one summary alert, a review step before confirm, an edit form that sends only changed fields with the version it opened at (the server's counter of every accepted edit), a details view and a discard confirmation. Members see the list and details read-only. Compute is one entry for every member in the Deck bar and the Classic account menu and palette (`rail.js`, `palette.js`).
- **Promises:**

  - Every state says what it is: loading, empty (owner and member, hidden while a form is open), failed load with a reload button, image list unavailable (creation disabled with the reason), each status with its sentence, and "Not started yet" until the lifecycle exists. Copy about running or enforcement is conditional ("Once workspaces can start, ..."); a saved record says "Saved in Commonplace. Nothing has been sent to the machine yet." Nothing is sent before the confirm step; a retry after a lost answer reuses the request id. Errors from the server land beside their field. All user text is escaped; there are no inline styles; targets are at least 44px and inputs 16px on the phone, the actions stay above the fixed tab bar and the page never scrolls sideways at 390px; focus moves to the panel heading on open, to the confirmation line after a save and back to the opening button on close or Escape; Deck control borders are at least 3:1.
- **How it fails:**

  - A failed or unanswered save keeps the person's values and says whether anything is confirmed; a form opened before the list changed refuses to send and says why; a 401 ends the visit like the rest of Compute; a 403 for the owner inventory still shows the read-only list.
- **Tests:** `tests/compute_workspaces_ui.mjs` (via `tests/test_compute_workspaces_ui.py`) and `tests/test_compute_workspaces_browser.py` (Classic and Deck, desktop and phone, owner and member).
