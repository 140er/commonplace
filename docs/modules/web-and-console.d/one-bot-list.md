- **Owns:** `routes.js` resolves the older workspace-wide conversations addresses in Deck; `bots.js`, `deck-coworkers.js`, `deck.js` and `deck-shell.js` carry the labels. `#/bots` is the one list of bots in Deck.
- **Promises:** Every row on `#/bots` opens that bot (its thread, or its detail for a bot only the Coworker registry knows). A bot's conversations, setup ("Ready to start?"), work in progress, steering and sharing are reached from the bot: its menu offers "Conversations and setup", which opens `#/bots/<bot-id>/coworkers`, a page titled by the bot with a "Conversations" tab. In Deck, `#/bots/-/coworkers` and the older `#/coworkers` resolve to `#/bots` (replacing the history entry, so Back does not loop); the conversation addresses `#/bots/-/coworkers/conversations[/<id>][?message=<id>]` and `#/bots/<bot-id>/coworkers` are unchanged. The "New bot" action stays on the list and on the bot's conversations page. Classic keeps `#/bots/-/coworkers` and its layout. No second page or phone title says "Bot conversations": the phone Bots tab and the sidebar Bots heading both open `#/bots`, and the conversations pages are titled "Conversations".
- **How it fails:** A bot id that is unknown still renders its conversations page without a parent, as before; it never falls back to a list of other bots. Reads, steering and sharing keep their existing authority checks.
- **Tests:** `deck_workspace_pages.mjs` pins the redirect (Deck) and the unchanged classic address; `deck_shell.mjs` the phone title; `deck_coworker_empty_states.mjs` the bot-scoped page; `test_deck_shell_browser.py` and `test_deck_phone_navigation_browser.py` the live routes on desktop and phone.
