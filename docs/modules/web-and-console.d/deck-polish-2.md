- **Owns:** `deck-memory.js` (`excerpt`, `resultWords`, `sourcesWarning`) and `memory.js` show each Memory result as a window of its words around the first match with a "Show full text" toggle, and name the sources that did not answer; each failed source in `searchMemory` now carries a `reason` (timeout, busy, warming, denied, failed or skipped). `rail.js` puts the project name in its own element and `workbench.css` keeps the classic phone tab bar pinned to the foot of the screen.
- **Promises:** A result over about 220 characters shows a window of the stored words that includes the first match; the stored text is never changed, and "Show full text" opens it whole and "Show less" cuts it again without opening the source. The warning reads "Some sources could not be searched: notes (no answer in time); Product chat and cards (the server had a problem)" and keeps its Try again button. In the classic look on a phone the tab bar sits at the foot of the screen on a short page and while a long page scrolls, and a long project name is cut with an ellipsis instead of being broken inside a word.
- **How it fails:** A response that carries no reason (an older page state) gives the warning without the parenthesis. The classic bottom bar still lists only Bots and Memory; the other destinations stay in the top-left menu.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_deck_polish2_browser.py` runs the real shell against a served fixture (skips where no browser is installed) and checks the cut results and toggle in both looks, the named sources, the pinned bar on four classic routes and the unbroken project name at 390 px and 360 px, red on the previous build; `tests/deck_memory.mjs` pins the excerpt, the warning text and the failure reasons.
