- **Promises:** The Deck's Waiting on you page (`#/needs` resolves to it) lists each machine setup step that waits on the owner as a consent card, under All and Reviews, and counts it with the cards and config proposals, so the header count and the list always agree. The card, its fingerprint check and its refuse step are the classic Needs me ones: one shared handler in `runnerconsent.js` serves both screens, and a card keeps its own confirm, error and result state across a refresh.
- **How it fails:** A decision that cannot be saved leaves the card in place with "Could not save. Refresh Needs me to check the current setup before trying again."; a refused machine drops its other pending steps from the list.
