- **Owns:**

  - The Compute map screen at `#/compute-map` (`topology-map.js` with `topology-layout.js` and `topology-icons.js`, styled by `topology.css`): an SVG canvas with pan, zoom, dragged positions and a highlighted site route. It draws only what `GET /api/topology` returns, nodes, links, routes and the `status` word per node and link end, reads again every five seconds while visible, and keeps only dragged positions and the view in the browser per workspace. Contract: `docs/topology.md`, "The map in the app".

  - The Compute map in the Deck look: `deck-compute.js` is the only map there. `#/compute` mounts the same canvas (`topology-map.js`, `topology-layout.js`, `topology-icons.js`) from `GET /api/topology` inside one card, with the legend under it and the selected node's detail view (the four tabs of the node panel) as a second card; `#/compute-map` resolves to `#/compute` and the account menu has no second map entry. The Deck shell's refresh drives the reads (the canvas runs with `interval: 0`), and the chosen node survives a refresh. When the topology read is empty or fails, the screen draws the hub and the enrolled runner machines from `GET /api/runners` through the same canvas, in the states Ready, Waiting for attestation and Off, with no claim of liveness, sites or routes; its Config tab comes from the runner row (projects, topics, wings, longest runtime mode, trust "attested by the owner, not verified"). A non-owner gets one plain sentence. Add machine goes to Admin; Add site and "Let a bot map it" store a draft for the general bot in `sessionStorage` (`cmmn.deck.draft`) and open it.

  - The canvas options a host such as this one uses: `interval: 0`, `refresh()`, a quiet `select(key)`, and a `look` (status words and legend, "no mark" status, label width with a plate behind each label in the card colour, columns per row, host-described detail tabs, a first selection). The Deck topology case uses `layeredLayout` (rows with a free lane for each link that passes a row) and `topology-route.js`, which routes every link on a grid around the device discs and label plates in eight directions, so no link runs through a device or a label; a link whose straight line is clear stays straight, and a map of more than 80 devices keeps straight links. In the Deck look a label is drawn at a constant size on the screen (12px name, 11px second line) at every zoom, its plate sized for a view of 0.85 or more; the first view fits the graph's width (never below 0.85) and the card is as tall as the graph with its padding, so the page scrolls rather than the card (the card caps at two screens and then pans); a name or second line wraps to two lines before anything is cut; a row holds as many devices as fit the card width at the plate width (five on a desktop, two on a phone). The classic look is unchanged.

- **Promises:**

  - A status is never shown by colour alone (shape and text as well), a stale or unrecorded device is drawn as such instead of dropped or invented, and nothing is loaded from another origin.

  - Capy Compute presentation lives in `deck-compute-capy.js` and `deck-compute-capy.css`. It keeps the shared canvas, drag/pan/zoom, link selection and Management tab. `deck-compute-scene.js` supplies 48px card geometry, 28px desktop icon tiles, single-line phone names and straight centre-to-centre links, curving only obstructed pairs without the grid router. End dots, loose bundle counts and the legend are omitted in this view. Fit and Reset are quiet icon buttons inside the canvas; the route picker lives in Management and returns its selected route to the map. The title and status precede the tabs. The fitted graph is centred with 48px padding and a 360px desktop minimum. The inspector is 300px on desktop; the phone follows recorded directed links in a vertical tree and opens only the selected device's inspector, with a Back control. The original detail tabs remain under Device details. The inspector maps recorded facts only: capacity alone is labelled capacity, usage meters require usage and total, unavailable fields hide, and runner enrollment readiness never implies health. Add a machine links to existing workspace administration enrollment; it never starts a second setup flow. Link method is carried through to the SVG: recorded Tailscale is cyan, recorded LAN is violet, and other current links are cyan at 55% opacity; missing or stale links are grey. Link strokes are 1px dashed, with capitalized 11px labels on stage-colour backplates near their midpoints. Only links with two live ends animate (4/6 dash, 1.4s); expired, missing and idle links stay still, as does every link under reduced motion. The classic look uses its original styles and panel renderer.
