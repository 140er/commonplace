- **Owns:**

  - `tests/topology_harness.html` (with `topology_harness.mjs` and `topology_harness.css`) and `tests/topology_shots.sh` are the screenshot harness for the Compute map: the real `topology-map.js` and stylesheets over a scripted API serving `tests/topology_fixture.mjs`, an invented topology in the shape of `GET /api/topology`. Its walk scene drives the real DOM with pointer and wheel events and the script fails unless every check of the walk passed. `tests/topology_map_view.mjs` (run by `tests/test_topology_map_view.py`) holds the canvas's node-level checks. `tests/test_topology_graph_status.py` covers the whole-graph read's `status` block: one word per node and link end, expired status as unknown, a stale node's last label in `fields`, the empty workspace creating no file, and workspace isolation.

  - `tests/commonplace_deck_compute.mjs` (run by `tests/test_commonplace_deck_compute.py`) drives the real Deck Compute screen over a scripted API: the canvas draws a topology read, an empty or failed read falls back to the runners in honest states, selecting a node opens exactly one detail view, the choice survives a refresh, and the geometry of the fallback graphs (a hub with 1, 2, 5, 8 and 12 machines, short and very long names, a desktop and a phone field) keeps every label whole, apart from the others and clear of every status mark.

  - `tests/deck_compute_capy.mjs`, wrapped by `tests/test_deck_compute_capy.py`, checks inspector field mapping and absence, plain label copy, zero values, capacity versus usage, escaping, live versus idle links, motion preference changes and cleanup, existing enrollment navigation, and the phone tree. `tests/test_deck_compute_capy_browser.py` optionally exercises the real Deck controllers at desktop and phone sizes, checking the inspector width, no horizontal page overflow, idle labels, touch targets, reduced motion and enrollment navigation. It skips without the browser dependency or executable, or when `COMMONPLACE_BROWSER_TESTS=off`; screenshot output is optional via `CAPY_COMPUTE_SHOTS`.

  - Approved Compute refinement checks cover straight and obstructed links, no end marks or loose counts, 48px nodes, single-line phone names, capitalized midpoint labels and the two canvas controls, distinct node-status geometry with accessible words, panel-marker fallback and workspace-tab restoration (including a removed reference sibling). Browser captures include a selected link, alongside desktop, phone and phone-inspector states.
