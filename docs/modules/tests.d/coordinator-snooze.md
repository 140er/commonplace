  - `tests/needs_me_cards.mjs` checks that an older queue entry cannot keep a newer released card waiting, while current, newer and unversioned queue entries retain their authority.
  - `tests/test_card_action_refresh_browser.py` holds the owner queue refresh across an origin release and checks that the fresh card panel shows Ready before the queue answers and stays Ready afterward.
