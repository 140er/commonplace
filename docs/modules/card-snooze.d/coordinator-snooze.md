- **Owns:** Snooze reasons stored verbatim in the additive nullable `card_snoozes.reason` column. Existing rows survive migration with no reason. The card payload and display text include the reason, as do history and the `card.snoozed` event.
- **Promises:** The existing coordinator connection and live board lease checks govern coordinator snooze and unsnooze. Authority is limited to that board's Backlog/Todo cards; snoozing through coordinator authority requires nonempty text of at most 500 characters. Owner and assignee reasons remain optional. REST uses the same service rules as the tools.
- **How it fails:** Missing, blank, non-text or oversized coordinator reasons refuse without changing the card. Former coordinators, other connections and other boards gain no authority. The workspace owner can undo any coordinator snooze.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_coordinator_card_snooze.py` covers both entry points, reason validation, lease loss, history, migration and stall suppression.
