- **Owns:** `COMMONPLACE_SIGNUP_ALLOW_EMAILS` lets named people create an account while `COMMONPLACE_SIGNUP` is closed. `social/accounts.py` parses it (`allowed_signup_emails`, at most 20 entries, split on commas and whitespace) and matches it (`allowlisted_email`); `_resolve_tx` applies it to every provider sign-up, and `email_auth.py` lets a closed instance send a sign-in link to a listed address.
- **Promises:** A closed instance still refuses everyone else exactly as before. A sign-up succeeds only when a provider-verified email equals an entry after strip and casefold: no plus-address stripping, no domain wildcards, no partial match, no other claim. The account takes the listed address. Unset or empty changes nothing, open mode and existing people are unchanged, and the account cap still applies.
- **How it fails:** Extra or malformed entries are ignored with a warning that only counts them; the list and the matched email are never logged, returned or put in an error, only the fixed text `allowlisted signup`. An unverified provider email never reaches the match, and a link checked after the list changed is refused.
