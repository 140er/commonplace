  - The lifecycle write shape on the existing key recovery route.

- **Promises:**

  - The explicit inventory describes recovery separately from enrolled-key lifecycle reporting. A lifecycle signature grants no person decision or credential recovery capability after revocation.

  - Foreign keys, token callers, replay, changed identity and invalid issuer evidence are refused. Off-state public surfaces retain their pins.
