- **Owns:** `commonplace/chat/coworkers/livelog.py` projects scoped job output; `steps.py` projects the working-on tree; `_util.py` shares bounded numeric parsing without route imports. The complete API contract and examples live in `docs/system/coworker-live-log.md`.
- **Promises:** Cookie-person, conversation and current project authority fence every read; markup, escapes and controls normalize before shared redaction, path masking and truncation. Tails retain at most 200 lines of 500 characters from 20 jobs; detail, tail and stream live projections share one rate budget and eight-reader pool, without retained subscribers or new output storage.
- **How it fails:** Invalid or foreign-scope cursors are rejected, stale cursors reset the tail, revoked or rebound sources disclose no output or run steps, and oversized sources are omitted. Busy or rate-limited log routes return 429; authorized detail stays 200 with `limited: true` and no live fields, after rechecking viewer/session and project authority. Unreported output remains an empty log; ledger pointers and runner files are never opened.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_coworker_livelog_contract.py` pins the HTTP contract; `tests/test_coworker_livelog.py` covers redaction, isolation, binding, bounds, cursors and states; `tests/test_coworker_livelog_limits.py` pins shared detail/log admission, graceful degradation, fallback authority and single-segment GET dispatch.
