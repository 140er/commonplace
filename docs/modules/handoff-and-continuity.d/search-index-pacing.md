- **Owns:** The search-index sweep takes small steps by default: 3 conversation threads and 192 message units per sweep with a 3 s rest between sweeps (before: 20 threads, 2000 units, 1 s). `COMMONPLACE_SEARCH_INDEX_THREADS`, `_UNITS` and `_PAUSE` override them within fixed bounds, and invalid values fall back to the default. The sweep thread runs at a lower CPU priority (`COMMONPLACE_SEARCH_INDEX_NICE`, default 10, `0` leaves it alone). The sweep runs inside `foreground_load.background()` so its own calls never count as people.
- **Promises:** What is indexed and the verbatim text are unchanged; the backfill stays incremental and idempotent, only paced.
- **How it fails:** A worker refusal that is not a storage quota stops the sweep after that thread, counts a failure (`State.failures`) and doubles the rest before the next sweep (up to 60 s); a clean sweep resets it. The refused range stays pinned and is replayed on a later pass.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_search_index_contention.py` covers the small defaults, the clamped environment settings and the backoff after a failed write; `tests/test_search_index_sweep.py` covers the bounded jobs.
